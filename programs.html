Academics & Enrichment

Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum designed to challenge, inspire, and prepare every student for success.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-K program provides a nurturing environment for young learners, with experienced teachers and a play-based curriculum that fosters social, emotional, and cognitive growth.

📚

Elementary

Our elementary program offers a challenging and engaging education that prepares students for success in middle school and beyond. Our experienced teachers use a variety of teaching methods to meet the unique needs of each student.

🔬

Middle/Upper

Our middle and upper school programs offer a rigorous education that prepares students for success in college and beyond. We offer a wide range of courses and extracurricular activities that help our students develop important life skills and build meaningful relationships with their peers.

💻

STEM

Our STEM program provides a rigorous education in science, technology, engineering, and math, with experienced teachers and state-of-the-art facilities that foster hands-on learning and innovation.

🎨

Arts

Our arts program offers a wide range of courses and extracurricular activities that help our students develop their creativity and self-expression. From music and drama to visual arts and dance, we provide opportunities for our students to explore their passions and talents.

🏈

Athletics

Our athletics program offers a wide range of sports and extracurricular activities that help our students develop important life skills, such as teamwork, discipline, and perseverance. We compete in the Arizona Interscholastic Association (AIA) and offer a variety of teams for boys and girls.
