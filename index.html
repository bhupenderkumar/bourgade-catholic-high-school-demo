Why Families Choose Us

Bourgade Catholic High School — The Difference

Discover why families across Phoenix trust us with their children's education.

👩‍🏫

Expert Faculty

Our faculty and staff are committed to providing a high-quality education that meets the unique needs of each student. With years of experience and a passion for teaching, we are dedicated to helping our students succeed.

📚

Small Class Sizes

Our small class sizes ensure that each student receives the individual attention they need to excel academically and personally. With a student-to-teacher ratio of 12:1, we provide a personalized education that is tailored to the needs of each student.

🏫

Safe Campus

Our campus is a safe and welcoming environment that provides a sense of community and belonging for our students. With state-of-the-art facilities and a commitment to security, we ensure that our students feel safe and supported on campus.

📊

Rigorous Curriculum

Our curriculum is designed to challenge our students and prepare them for success in college and beyond. With a focus on critical thinking, problem-solving, and communication skills, we provide a well-rounded education that prepares our students for the demands of the 21st century.

👫

Whole-Child Approach

We believe that education is about more than just academics. Our whole-child approach focuses on the social, emotional, and spiritual development of our students, providing them with the tools they need to succeed in all areas of life.

🌎

Community Engagement

We are committed to community engagement and service, providing our students with opportunities to make a positive impact in the world. From volunteer work to service projects, we encourage our students to use their talents and skills to make a difference.

Our Programs

A comprehensive curriculum that challenges, inspires, and prepares every student.

🎒

Pre-Kindergarten

Our Pre-K program provides a nurturing environment for young learners, with experienced teachers and a play-based curriculum that fosters social, emotional, and cognitive growth.

📚

Elementary

Our elementary program offers a challenging and engaging education that prepares students for success in middle school and beyond. Our experienced teachers use a variety of teaching methods to meet the unique needs of each student.

🔬

Middle/Upper

Our middle and upper school programs offer a rigorous education that prepares students for success in college and beyond. We offer a wide range of courses and extracurricular activities that help our students develop important life skills and build meaningful relationships with their peers.
